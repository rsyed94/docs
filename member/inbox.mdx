Open in Passport: [Inbox](https://passportmcp.com/#inbox)

**Inbox** is the one place for everything that needs you. It was the **Approvals** page, and old **Approvals** links still open it. It has four sections, always in this order, and an empty section shows one quiet line instead of hiding the others.

## Waiting on you

Decisions only you can make right now, routed to you first, then oldest first:

- **Agent actions** your [Autopilot rules](/member/safety) ask about, or that an admin routed to you. Each row says who asked, the action and its class (Read, Change, Delete, or Irreversible), the environment, how long it has waited, and your choices: **Approve once**, **Approve for this session**, **Always allow**, or **Decline**.
- **Calls on a shared account** that need an approver.
- **App requests**, **tool access** requests, and **rule requests** (exceptions) from your team, if you're an admin. See [Catalog access, passes, and requests](/admin/approvals-and-requests) and [exception requests](/admin/slack-and-approvers#exception-requests).

On a phone, a request opens on its own screen with **Approve** and **Decline**, and **Next request** moves through the rest. See [On your phone](/member/safety#on-your-phone).

## Needs attention

Things that are broken or about to stop working, most severe first. Each row is one link to the page where you fix it:

| What | Where it opens |
| --- | --- |
| A computer still holds keys Passport could hold | The app's **Keys** section, such as [Railway's](https://passportmcp.com/#apps/railway/keys), or [Apps](https://passportmcp.com/#apps) for keys of several apps |
| One of your agents has a problem, such as its guard is off or a key came back | That agent's page |
| An app needs you to sign in again | The app's page, with **Sign in again** |
| An app can't connect for anyone (admins) | The app's page |
| An app added or changed tools that stay off until reviewed (admins) | The app's **Tools** section, with **Review tools** |
| Critical data check findings nobody marked reviewed (Enterprise admins) | The finding's own page, or **Rules › Data checks** when there are several |

A data check finding leaves the Inbox when an admin opens it and chooses **Mark reviewed** on its page (`#rules/data-checks/<id>`). See [Review a finding](/admin/data-checks#review-a-finding).

Home's **Needs attention** shows these rows too, plus a few kinds the Inbox doesn't carry yet, such as environments to place, work someone asked you to review, and rule suggestions. See [Home](/member/home).

## Your requests

What you and your agents asked someone else for, newest first, each with a status: **Waiting**, or **Being set up** once an admin approved it and is still setting it up. That covers your agents' actions waiting on another approver, app requests, tool access requests (such as **Full access to** an app), and rule requests, including ones an agent filed for you. They never count as waiting on you.

## History

What was decided or lapsed in the last 30 days, newest first: **Approved**, **Declined**, **Expired**, **Withdrawn**, **Cancelled**, or **Answered**. When you both asked for things and decided other people's, filter it with **Decided for you** or **Decided by you**. **Open in All activity** shows anything older.

## The badge

The number beside **Inbox** in the navigation is **Waiting on you** plus **Needs attention**. Home's "things need attention" line uses the same number. Your requests and history never add to it.

The alerts bell is separate: it keeps security events, data check hits, and tool changes, and its footer links to the Inbox.

## Get notified

Turn on **Approval alerts in this browser** under **Notifications** in [Settings › Account](https://passportmcp.com/#settings) to get a notification for each request that waits on you. Admins can also send requests to Slack. See [Browser approval notifications](/admin/approvals-and-requests#browser-approval-notifications) and [Approvals in Slack](/admin/slack-and-approvers#approvals-in-slack).
