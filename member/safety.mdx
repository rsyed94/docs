title: "Safety preferences"
description: "Choose when Passport should ask before write or destructive actions."
---

Safety preferences are your personal brakes. They never give you more access than your workspace already allows — they only make things stricter for your own AI assistants.

## Where to find them

Open **Settings**. The **Safety** card is near the top of the page. You will see two choices:

| Preference | Choices | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Write actions | Allow / Ask me / Never | Allow |
| Destructive actions | Allow / Ask me / Never | Ask me |

Read-only actions are not controlled here. Workspace rules (who can use which app, and company guardrails) still apply first.

## What each choice means

When your AI assistant tries an action through Passport:

- **Allow** — go ahead, if your workspace already permits it.
- **Ask me** — pause and wait for your decision (allow once, allow for this session, or deny). An **Approvals** item appears in the navigation while something is waiting.
- **Never** — block it immediately.

Automation identities (agent keys) do not get Ask-me prompts — they are non-interactive. Use your normal member sign-in when a human should decide.

## How this works with company rules

Personal Safety sits on top of workspace access and guardrails:

- If your access is already read-only, write tools stay hidden no matter what Safety says.
- If a company guardrail would block a secret, Safety cannot override that.

Use Safety when you want a personal check before writes or risky actions, without changing rules for everyone else.
