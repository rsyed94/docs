Open in Passport: [Settings › Billing](https://passportmcp.com/#settings/billing)

Passport has three plans. Every plan includes unlimited apps, the full app catalog, Work, Agents, Apps, and Rules, Passport CLI, and Passport's one MCP server URL. Free is for one person. Pro is the team plan: People, teams, team rules, rollout, and the whole workspace's agents and record. Enterprise adds company-wide controls for identity, data checks, and streaming to your SIEM.

## How apps work on each plan

Verified catalog apps are open: choose **Add**, and Passport either finishes the connection or asks an admin when a shared company credential is required. On Enterprise, a workspace can review new apps first, and members see **Ask an admin to add**. There is no app limit on any plan.

On every plan, workspace admins can finish technical setup (company API keys, OAuth clients, custom URLs, removing apps). Built-in secret scanning and each person's rules also stay on every plan. Pro adds access and team minimums by team and person, and the workspace's record with export. Enterprise adds custom data checks and streaming to your SIEM.

Passport CLI is included on every plan and uses the same rules and record as every other agent.

## How seats are counted

Anyone who can sign in counts as a person (admin or member). Automations (the agent tokens scripts and CI use) are never people and are never billed.

- **Free:** one person and up to 3 automations.
- **Pro and Enterprise:** unlimited people and automations. Only people are billed.

On paid plans, Passport updates your Stripe subscription as people join or leave and prorates the difference. You do not manage seat quantities by hand.

## At the Free limit

Free is for one person. Inviting your first teammate starts a 30-day Pro trial for the whole team, with no card. After that, a Free workspace adds people only on Pro, and pending invites hold a place. A fourth automation waits the same way.

Upgrade during the trial and billing starts when the trial would have ended. A workspace gets one trial, and a workspace that has paid before doesn't get one.

If a workspace has more people than its plan allows, for example after a trial or a paid plan ends, everyone keeps access and nothing is deleted. Only new people wait.

<Note>
  Workspaces created on the earlier Free plan keep Free for up to 5 people, now with unlimited apps and up to 5
  automations that no longer use a person's place.
</Note>

## When a plan changes

Changing plans never deletes configuration. Settings that need a higher plan are kept but stop applying until that plan is active again.

- **Enterprise to Pro:** SSO, SCIM, custom data checks, domain joining, and streaming stop applying. People, teams, team rules, and the workspace's record keep working.
- **Pro to Free:** the People page and the workspace's record pause, and teams are kept. A saved team minimum keeps applying, since it only tightens. Team and person access rules keep restricting access until an owner resets the app to workspace-wide read-only or full.

## Upgrade, change, or cancel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upgrade">
    Open [Settings › Billing](https://passportmcp.com/#settings/billing) and upgrade. Stripe Checkout asks for a card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change plan">
    Plan changes on an existing subscription go through the Stripe customer portal. Passport edits your one subscription
    in place and prorates. It never opens a second checkout, so you are not double-billed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cancel">
    Cancel anytime from **Billing** in Settings, via the Stripe portal. You keep the paid plan until the period
    ends, then move to Free. Your data stays until you delete the workspace.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  On a deployment explicitly configured with `PASSPORT_BILLING_MODE=self-hosted`, billing is off and Enterprise is
  enabled for every workspace. See [Self-host](/self-host).
</Note>
