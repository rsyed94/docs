Passport has three plans. Every plan includes the full app catalog, Apps and Clients, your personal Activity log, Safety preferences, the desktop app (macOS), Passport CLI, and the shared connection path. Pro removes the Free caps. Enterprise adds company-wide controls for people, approvals, clients, guardrails, and audit export.

## How apps work on each plan

On Free and Pro, verified catalog apps are open: choose **Connect**, and Passport either finishes the connection or asks an admin when a shared company credential is required. Free's five-app limit counts **connected** apps, not everything you can browse. Pausing an app does not free a slot.

On Enterprise, admins can keep the catalog open, require review, or set rules by app, category, and team. Access usually starts read-only, and an app is not marked ready until required company setup is done.

On every plan, workspace admins can finish technical setup (company API keys, OAuth clients, custom URLs, removing apps). Built-in secret scanning and personal Safety preferences also stay on every plan. Enterprise adds custom guardrails, team and client rules, and company-wide audit export.

Passport CLI is included on every plan and uses the same rules and Activity log as other connections.

## How seats are counted

Anyone who can sign in counts as a person (admin or member).

- **Pro and Enterprise:** automation identities (agents) are **not** billed seats.
- **Free:** agents **do** use one of the five member slots.

On paid plans, Passport updates your Stripe subscription as people join or leave and prorates the difference. You do not manage seat quantities by hand.

## At the Free limit

Free includes 5 people and 5 connected apps. Agents count toward those five people. You can still browse the whole catalog. Adding a sixth person or agent, or connecting a sixth app, is blocked until you upgrade to Pro. Nothing is deleted, and everyone already on the workspace keeps working.

## Upgrade, change, or cancel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upgrade">
    Open **Settings**, find the **Billing** card (Free/Pro owners see **Plan & billing**), and upgrade. Stripe Checkout asks for a card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change plan">
    Plan changes on an existing subscription go through the Stripe customer portal. Passport edits your one subscription in place and prorates — it never opens a second checkout, so you are not double-billed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cancel">
    Cancel anytime from the **Billing** / **Plan & billing** card in Settings, via the Stripe portal. You keep the paid plan until the period ends, then move to Free. Your data stays until you delete the workspace.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  On a deployment explicitly configured with `PASSPORT_BILLING_MODE=self-hosted`, billing is off and every workspace is unlocked. See [Self-host](/self-host).
</Note>
