Passport has three plans. Every plan includes unlimited apps, the full app catalog, Apps and Agents, your personal Activity log, Safety preferences, the desktop app (macOS), Passport CLI, and the shared connection path. Free is for one person. Pro is for teams. Enterprise adds company-wide controls for people, approvals, agents, guardrails, and audit export.

## How apps work on each plan

On Free and Pro, verified catalog apps are open: choose **Connect**, and Passport either finishes the connection or asks an admin when a shared company credential is required. There is no app limit on any plan.

On Enterprise, admins can keep the catalog open, require review, or set rules by app, category, and team. Access usually starts read-only, and an app is not marked ready until required company setup is done.

On every plan, workspace admins can finish technical setup (company API keys, OAuth clients, custom URLs, removing apps). Built-in secret scanning and personal Safety preferences also stay on every plan. Enterprise adds custom guardrails, team and client rules, and company-wide audit export.

Passport CLI is included on every plan and uses the same rules and Activity log as other connections.

## How seats are counted

Anyone who can sign in counts as a person (admin or member). Automations (the agent keys scripts and CI use) are never people and are never billed.

- **Free:** one person and up to 3 automations.
- **Pro and Enterprise:** unlimited people and automations. Only people are billed.

On paid plans, Passport updates your Stripe subscription as people join or leave and prorates the difference. You do not manage seat quantities by hand.

## At the Free limit

Free is for one person. Inviting, importing, or approving a second person waits until you upgrade to Pro, and pending invites hold a place. A fourth automation waits the same way.

If a workspace has more people than its plan allows, for example after a trial or a paid plan ends, everyone keeps access and nothing is deleted. Only new people wait.

<Note>
  Workspaces created on the earlier Free plan keep Free for up to 5 people, now with unlimited apps and up to 5
  automations that no longer use a person's place.
</Note>

## Upgrade, change, or cancel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upgrade">
    Open **Settings**, find the **Billing** card (Free/Pro owners see **Plan & billing**), and upgrade. Stripe Checkout
    asks for a card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change plan">
    Plan changes on an existing subscription go through the Stripe customer portal. Passport edits your one subscription
    in place and prorates — it never opens a second checkout, so you are not double-billed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cancel">
    Cancel anytime from the **Billing** / **Plan & billing** card in Settings, via the Stripe portal. You keep the paid
    plan until the period ends, then move to Free. Your data stays until you delete the workspace.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  On a deployment explicitly configured with `PASSPORT_BILLING_MODE=self-hosted`, billing is off and every workspace is
  unlocked. See [Self-host](/self-host).
</Note>
