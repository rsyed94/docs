Open in Passport: [Work](https://passportmcp.com/#work)

Passport gives Claude, Cursor, ChatGPT, Codex, and other agents a safe way to use your work apps.

Connect GitHub, Linear, Stripe, and other apps once. Passport keeps the credentials, applies your rules, and records what happened. Your agent never receives the provider password, token, or key.

## The four places

Passport has four places in the left rail, or the bottom bar on a phone:

- [Work](/member/work): what's waiting on you, what's running, and the record of every task and action.
- [Agents](/member/agents): every agent that acts for you, whether it works, and how to add one.
- [Apps](/member/apps): the apps your agents can reach, who can use them, and where their resources sit.
- [Rules](/member/safety): what agents may do without asking, per environment.

Your name, at the bottom left, opens the account menu. **Settings** is there for everyone, and owners on Pro and Enterprise also get [People](/admin/people-and-teams). See [Settings](/admin/settings).

Links to older pages, such as Home, Inbox, or Sessions, still open the right place.
