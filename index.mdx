Open in Passport: [Home](https://passportmcp.com/#home)

Passport gives Claude, Cursor, ChatGPT, Codex, and other AI assistants a safe way to use your work apps.

Connect GitHub, Linear, Stripe, and other apps once. Passport keeps the credentials, applies your workspace rules, and records what happened. Your AI assistant never receives the provider password or token.

You do not need to know how MCP works. Most people can get started from [Apps](https://passportmcp.com/#apps) and [Agents](https://passportmcp.com/#agents) in a few minutes.

## The two parts of Passport

Most people use these pages:

- [Home](/member/home): what's running, what needs you, and your week
- [Inbox](/member/inbox): what waits on your decision, what needs fixing, and what you asked for
- **Sessions**: see what your agents did, with every action under **All activity**
- **Rules**: choose what agents do on their own and when they ask you
- **Apps**: add and connect work apps
- **Agents**: add Claude, Cursor, ChatGPT, Codex, or another agent
- [Settings](/admin/settings): your account, your keys, and where you're signed in

Pro and Enterprise admins also get **People** (with **Invitations** and **Adoption**), **Rules › Team**, **Apps › Setups**, and [Agents › Rollout](/admin/rollout), and switch **All activity** to **Everyone** to see the whole workspace. Enterprise adds **Agents › Discovered** and **Rules › Data checks**. Those controls do not change the everyday member flow.
