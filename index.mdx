Passport gives Claude, Cursor, ChatGPT, Codex, and other AI assistants a safe way to use your work apps.

Connect GitHub, Linear, Stripe, and other apps once. Passport keeps the credentials, applies your workspace rules, and records what happened. Your AI assistant never receives the provider password or token.

You do not need to know how MCP works. Most people can get started from **Apps** and **Clients** in a few minutes.

## The two parts of Passport

Most people use four pages:

- **Apps** — browse and connect work apps
- **Clients** — add Claude, Cursor, ChatGPT, Codex, or another AI client
- **Activity** — review your own actions
- **Settings** — manage Safety, sessions, and your account

Enterprise admins also get organization-wide pages for **People**, **Discover**, **Guardrails**, **Audit**, and **Settings**. Those controls do not change the everyday member flow.
