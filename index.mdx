Open in Passport: [Passport](https://passportmcp.com/)

Passport sits between your apps and Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, and ChatGPT. Agents never hold your keys, your rules decide what runs, and every action is on the record.

Connect GitHub, Railway, Stripe, and other apps once. Passport keeps the keys, applies your rules, and records what happened. Your agent never receives the provider password, token, or key.
