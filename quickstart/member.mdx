Open in Passport: [Apps](https://passportmcp.com/#apps)

This is the shortest path from sign-in to a working agent. It works on Windows, Linux, and macOS.

## 1. Sign in

Open your invite, or sign in at [passportmcp.com](https://passportmcp.com). If your company uses single sign-on, Passport sends you to your company's sign-in page.

Passport opens on [Work](/member/work). The left rail has **Work**, **Agents**, **Apps**, and **Rules**; **Settings** is in the menu under your name.

## 2. Connect a work app

Open [Apps](https://passportmcp.com/#apps).

- Choose **Connect** on an app you already have, or **Add app** to search for one. Each result shows **Add**, or **Ask an admin to add** when your workspace reviews new apps.
- Finish the provider's sign-in in the window that opens.
- If the app needs company setup first, Passport says so instead of pretending it's ready.

Your provider credentials stay in Passport. They're never copied into Claude, Cursor, ChatGPT, Codex, or another agent.

## 3. Add your agent

Open [Agents](https://passportmcp.com/#agents) and choose **Add agent**:

- **Coding agents on a computer**, such as Claude Code, Cursor, and Codex: run `npx passport-bridge@latest init` in a terminal on that computer. It finds the agents there and connects each one.
- **Claude.ai** or **ChatGPT**: follow the steps Passport shows and paste the URL it gives you.
- **A cloud agent**, or **A script or job**: Passport creates a token for it. See [Agents](/member/agents#add-an-agent).

Each agent signs you in through your browser the first time. Never paste a provider token into your agent.

## 4. See it work

Ask your agent:

> List my available Passport tools.

Then ask it to do something real. The agent shows **Active** on **Agents**, and its task shows on [Work](https://passportmcp.com/#work) under **Running**, then **Recent**. Open the task to see every action it took.

If an ask comes up, it waits under **Waiting on you** on Work, and often in your agent too. See [When Passport asks](/member/safety#when-passport-asks).

## When your agent asks you to connect again

If an app was never connected or its sign-in expired, your agent can show the next step where you're working:

- Some agents show a built-in **Connect** or **Reconnect** action.
- Others show a secure Passport link in the conversation. Open it, check that it uses your Passport domain, and finish the provider's sign-in.
- Return to the agent when Passport says **All set**. The agent can retry; it never receives your provider password or token.

The link is short-lived and tied to your Passport account. Another signed-in person can't use it to connect their account on your behalf.

## Make it yours

- Open [Rules](/member/safety) to choose what agents do on their own and when they ask you.
- Open [Work › History](https://passportmcp.com/#work?view=history) to search everything your agents did.
