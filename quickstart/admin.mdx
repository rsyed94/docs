Open in Passport: [Home › Get started](https://passportmcp.com/#home)

Start here if you created the workspace. You will connect one useful app, prove the setup yourself, and then invite other people.

Free keeps administration inside [Settings](/admin/settings). Pro adds **People** (once someone else joins), team rules on **Rules › Team**, **Setups** on **Apps**, [Rollout](/admin/rollout) on **Agents**, and **Everyone** views of **Sessions › All activity** and **Agents**. Enterprise adds **Agents › Discovered** and **Rules › Data checks**.

Home's **Get started** checklist tracks the same first steps: connect an agent, add an app, pick your rules preset, and run one action. See [Home](/member/home#get-started).

## 1. Connect an app

Open [Apps › Add an app](https://passportmcp.com/#apps/add).

- Pick an app you already use, or search for it.
- Choose **Add**. The dialog explains any setup step, such as a shared key or an OAuth client.
- Complete your own provider sign-in, or finish any company setup Passport asks for.

Every app has its own page, even before it's added, with what it can do and the tools it will expose.

Verified catalog apps are self-service on Free and Pro. Enterprise can keep that flow open, require review, or apply catalog rules by app, category, publisher, and team.

## 2. Add your agent

Open [Agents › Add agent](https://passportmcp.com/#agents/add) and follow the instructions Passport shows. The normal path uses the agent's own support for remote MCP servers and browser sign-in.

Ask the agent to list its Passport tools, then choose **Test connection** in the setup guide. Passport waits for the agent's first request and says what it can reach. Home's **Run one action** step turns **Done** once the agent makes a successful call.

Do this before inviting the team. It proves the full connection and gives you a known-good example.

## 3. Invite people

- **Free:** inviting your first teammate starts a 30-day Pro trial for your team, with no card. After it, upgrade to Pro from the **Billing** card in [Settings](https://passportmcp.com/#settings) to add more people.
- **Pro and Enterprise:** open **People** and choose **Invite people**. Pending invites and join requests are on [People › Invitations](/admin/invitations).

Add people individually or in a batch. Invites are tied to the intended email address and expire after 14 days. On Pro and Enterprise, assign roles and teams as part of the rollout.

See [People and teams](/admin/people-and-teams) for roles, deactivation, teams, and automation credentials. To put Passport in every agent across the company, use [Agents › Rollout](/admin/rollout).

## 4. Choose the right level of control

You can start simple:

- Personal [Autopilot rules](/member/safety) are available on every plan.
- Built-in secret detection protects every plan.
- Pro adds teams, access rules and approval floors by team and person, Team setups, every agent in the workspace on **Agents › Everyone**, and workspace-wide Activity with export.
- Enterprise adds catalog review and rollout rules, agent policy, **Agents › Discovered** with scans for agents set up outside Passport, runtime security, custom data checks, SSO, SCIM, and streaming to your SIEM.

Start with the default behavior, watch real usage in **All activity**, and add controls when there is a clear need.

<Note>
  Everyone can see their own actions in **Sessions › All activity**. Pro and Enterprise admins switch the same tab to
  **Everyone** to review and export company-wide Activity, and can review privileged admin changes under
  [Settings › Workspace › Admin activity](https://passportmcp.com/#settings/workspace).
</Note>
