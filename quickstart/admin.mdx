title: "Set up a workspace"
description: "Connect the first app, add your agent, invite people, and confirm the setup."
---

Start here if you created the workspace. You will connect one useful app, prove the setup yourself, and then invite other people.

Free keeps administration inside **Settings**. Pro adds **People** (once someone else joins), team rules on **Rules → Team**, **Setups** on **Apps**, **Rollout** on **Agents**, and **Everyone** views of **Sessions → All activity** and **Agents**. Enterprise adds **Agents → Discovered** and **Rules → Data checks**.

## 1. Connect an app

Open **Apps**, then **Browse apps**.

- Pick an app you already use.
- Choose **Connect**.
- Complete your own provider sign-in, or finish any company setup Passport asks for.

Verified catalog apps are self-service on Free and Pro. Enterprise can keep that flow open, require review, or apply catalog rules by app, category, publisher, and team.

## 2. Add your agent

Open **Agents**, choose **Add agent**, and follow the instructions Passport shows. The normal path uses the agent's own support for remote MCP servers and browser sign-in.

Ask the agent to list its Passport tools, then return to **Agents** and choose **Run test call**. Passport opens **All activity** when the safe check succeeds.

Do this before inviting the team. It proves the full connection and gives you a known-good example.

## 3. Invite people

- **Free:** Free is for one person. Upgrade to Pro from **Settings → Billing** to invite your team.
- **Pro and Enterprise:** open **People**.

Add people individually or in a batch. Invites are tied to the intended email address and expire after 14 days. On Pro and Enterprise, assign roles and teams as part of the rollout.

See [People and teams](/admin/people-and-teams) for roles, deactivation, teams, and automation credentials.

## 4. Choose the right level of control

You can start simple:

- Personal [Autopilot rules](/member/safety) are available on every plan.
- Built-in secret detection protects every plan.
- Pro adds teams, access rules and approval floors by team and person, Team setups, every agent in the workspace on **Agents → Everyone**, and the workspace activity log with export.
- Enterprise adds catalog review and rollout rules, agent policy, **Agents → Discovered** with scans for connections outside Passport, runtime security, custom data checks, SSO, SCIM, and audit streaming.

Start with the default behavior, watch real usage in **All activity**, and add controls when there is a clear need.

<Note>
  Everyone can see their own actions in **Sessions → All activity**. Pro and Enterprise admins switch the same tab to
  **Everyone** to review and export company-wide activity, and can review privileged admin changes under **Settings →
  Admin activity**.
</Note>
