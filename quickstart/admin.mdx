Open in Passport: [Apps](https://passportmcp.com/#apps)

Start here if you created the workspace. Connect one useful app, prove the setup yourself, then invite other people.

On Free, you're the workspace's admin. On Pro and Enterprise, admins are **owners**: they also get **People** in the account menu, and **Mine** or **Everyone** on Agents.

## 1. Connect an app

Open [Apps](https://passportmcp.com/#apps) and choose **Add app**.

- Search for an app you already use and choose **Add**.
- Finish your own sign-in, or the setup step Passport asks for, such as a shared key or an OAuth client. The app's page has **Set up** for that.

Verified catalog apps are self-service on Free and Pro. See [Apps](/member/apps).

## 2. Add your agent

Open [Agents](https://passportmcp.com/#agents) and choose **Add agent**. For coding agents, run the one command it shows on your computer:

```sh
npx passport-bridge@latest init
```

Ask the agent to do something, then check that it shows **Active** on Agents. Do this before inviting the team. It proves the whole connection and gives you a known-good example.

## 3. Invite people

On Pro and Enterprise, open **People** from the menu under your name and choose **Invite people**. Paste as many addresses as you like, pick a role, and Passport emails each one an invite. See [People](/admin/people-and-teams).

## 4. Add controls

Start with the defaults and watch real work. Add controls when there's a clear need:

- Built-in secret detection protects every plan.
- Pro adds **Who can use it** by person on each app, and every agent on **Agents › Everyone**.
- Enterprise adds [single sign-on](/enterprise/sso-oidc), [SCIM](/enterprise/scim), and [streaming to your SIEM](/enterprise/siem-export).
