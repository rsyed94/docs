If you are setting up Passport for your team, start here. You will connect one app, add an AI assistant, invite people, and confirm everything works.

Free and Pro keep workspace management light under **Settings → Workspace essentials**. Enterprise keeps the same personal setup under **For me** / **My clients**, and adds company-wide pages for **People**, **Guardrails**, **Audit**, and **Settings**.

## Create your workspace

Sign up at [passportmcp.com](https://passportmcp.com) and confirm your email, or [self-host](/self-host) your own deployment.

You become the workspace admin. By default, joining is invite-only — nobody else appears until you add them.

## 1. Connect your first app

Open **Apps** and connect something useful:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="From the catalog" icon="grid">
    Pick a verified app and choose **Connect**. On Free and Pro this is usually one step. Enterprise can keep that open or require review.
  </Card>
  <Card title="By URL" icon="link">
    Point Passport at any tool server by URL. Passport connects, finds its tools, and scans them.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

New catalog apps start **read-only**, so an AI assistant cannot see write actions a person is not allowed to use. For approval rules and rollout, see [Catalog access and passes](/admin/approvals-and-requests).

## 2. Add an AI assistant

Open **Clients → Add client**. On Windows, Linux, or macOS, use the setup Passport shows, run `passport login`, or paste the connection URL.

The connection URL looks like `/w/ws_…/mcp`. The macOS desktop app can configure several local assistants at once; there is no Windows or Linux desktop app. Details: [member quickstart](/quickstart/member).

## 3. Invite your team

- **Free and Pro:** **Settings → Workspace essentials → Manage workspace**
- **Enterprise:** **People** (roles, teams, and company-wide policy)

See [People and teams](/admin/people-and-teams).

## 4. Check that everything works

On **Clients** (or **Getting started** during first-run setup), open the checklist and choose **Run setup check** on **Prove the governed path**.

Passport runs a safe test (no call out to the app provider). When it succeeds, your activity log opens so you can see the row (**Activity** for members; **Audit** in the Enterprise admin nav). If something is wrong, the step stays incomplete and you can retry.

<Note>
  Everyone can see their own Activity on every plan. Company-wide CSV/JSONL export and the admin audit trail are Enterprise. See [Activity and audit](/admin/activity-and-audit).
</Note>
