---
title: "Set up a workspace"
description: "Connect the first app, add your AI client, invite people, and confirm the setup."
---

Start here if you created the workspace. You will connect one useful app, prove the setup yourself, and then invite other people.

Free and Pro keep administration inside **Settings**. Enterprise adds organization-wide pages for **People**, **Discover**, **Guardrails**, **Audit**, and **Settings**.

## 1. Connect an app

Open **Apps**, then **Browse apps**.

- Pick an app you already use.
- Choose **Connect**.
- Complete your own provider sign-in, or finish any company setup Passport asks for.

Verified catalog apps are self-service on Free and Pro. Enterprise can keep that flow open, require review, or apply catalog rules by app, category, publisher, and team.

## 2. Add your AI client

Open **Clients**, choose **Add client**, and follow the instructions Passport shows. The normal path uses the AI client's own remote MCP support and browser sign-in; no Passport desktop app is required.

Ask the client to list its Passport tools, then return to **Clients** and choose **Run setup check**. Passport opens **Activity** when the safe check succeeds.

Do this before inviting the team. It proves the full connection and gives you a known-good example.

## 3. Invite people

- **Free and Pro:** open **Settings → Workspace essentials → Manage workspace**.
- **Enterprise:** open **People**.

Add people individually or in a batch. Invites are tied to the intended email address and expire after 14 days. On Enterprise, assign roles and teams as part of the rollout.

See [People and teams](/admin/people-and-teams) for roles, deactivation, teams, and agent identities.

## 4. Choose the right level of control

You can start simple:

- Personal [Safety preferences](/member/safety) are available on every plan.
- Built-in secret detection protects every plan.
- Enterprise adds company-wide app rollout, approvals, client policy, Discover, Runtime Security, custom guardrails, and audit export.

Start with the default behavior, watch real usage in **Activity** or **Audit**, and add controls when there is a clear need.

<Note>
  Everyone can see their own Activity. Enterprise admins can review and export company-wide Activity from **Audit**, and can review privileged admin changes under **Settings → Admin activity**.
</Note>
