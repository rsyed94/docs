Open in Passport: [People › Invitations](https://passportmcp.com/#people/invitations)

**People › Invitations** lists everyone who hasn't joined yet, in two groups. It's a tab beside **People**, **Teams**, and **Adoption**, for admins on Pro and Enterprise.

## Invite people

**Invite people** in the page header is the one way to invite. Add people one at a time or paste a CSV (`name, email, title, team`), choose admin or member, and Passport emails each an invite. See [Add people](/admin/people-and-teams#add-people).

Each invite is tied to the address it was sent to and expires after 14 days. The email also has one line to set up agents from a terminal, `npx passport-bridge init --org <key>`; an invitee who signs in or runs it with the invited address joins without the link.

## Join requests

People who asked to join the workspace come first. Each row shows who asked, how they signed in (**Email** or **Single sign-on**), and when.

- **Approve** adds them as a member and emails them a sign-in link. If that email can't be delivered, Passport says so; they're still approved.
- **Deny request**, in the row's menu, asks first, then removes the request.

## Pending invites

Invites that haven't been accepted, newest first. Each row shows who was invited, their team, who sent it, when, and when it expires.

- **Resend** sends a fresh invite email with a new link, and the old link stops working. Resending is limited to once a minute per invite.
- **Revoke invite**, in the row's menu, cancels the invite, so its link stops working.

With nothing pending, the tab says "No pending invites". Every approve, deny, resend, and revoke is recorded in [Admin activity](/admin/settings#workspace).

## Where else invites show

- **Needs attention** at the top of the People list shows join requests to review, with **Review** opening this tab.
- The People list's **Invited** filter (`#people?status=invited`) links here with **Open Invitations**.
- ⌘K finds **People › Invitations**.
- [Agents › Rollout](/admin/rollout) counts invites in its **Progress** bars.

On Free, inviting your first teammate starts a 30-day Pro trial for your team. See [Plans and billing](/billing#at-the-free-limit).
