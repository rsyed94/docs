Open in Passport: [Rules](https://passportmcp.com/#rules?scope=everyone)

Passport asks a person only when a rule says so. These settings decide who that person is and where they answer.

## Approvals in Slack

An admin adds the Passport app to Slack with **Add to Slack** under **Slack** in [Settings › Notifications](https://passportmcp.com/#settings/notifications). After that:

- Each ask arrives as a direct message to the person who can answer it, as the same ask Passport shows: who wants to do what, its environment, the agent, the command with secrets removed, why Passport asked, and what happens if it goes wrong.
- **Run once**, **Run for this task**, **It's Staging, always run** (when it applies), and **Don't run** work right in Slack. **Stop {agent}** and **Always allow…** (which opens Passport) are in the message's **⋯** menu. A Production change or delete, or anything irreversible, shows **Review in Passport** instead of Run once, because approving it takes a passkey check ([Confirm it's you](/member/safety#confirm-its-you)).
- Admins can also post Production asks to a **Production channel**. Anyone can see them there; only people allowed to approve can answer.
- When an ask is answered anywhere, every Slack message for it updates in place, such as "Approved once by Maya in Slack".

Passport finds each person in Slack by their Passport email. If Slack has no account with that email, Settings says so and asks still arrive in Passport. Each person can turn Slack messages off for themselves. If the app is removed from Slack, Settings says so and an admin can reconnect it. A self-hosted Passport needs the Slack app configured by whoever runs it.

## Production approvers

**Rules** shows one line under the table about who approves Production asks, such as "Production approvers: Maya. Someone other than the requester must approve." Members see their own version, such as "You can approve your own agents' Production asks."

Owners switch Rules to **Everyone** and choose **Change** to set:

- **Who approves**: **Workspace admins**, **One person**, or **Any of a few people**. A workspace with an on-call rotation can keep **Whoever is on call**.
- **Which asks**: **Deletes and irreversible actions**, or **Changes, deletes, and irreversible actions**.
- **Someone other than the requester must approve**.

Routed people get a notification and, with Slack connected, a direct message. The person whose agent asked sees who it's waiting on, like "Waiting on Maya". Nobody is routed their own ask. Teams that had their own routes keep them, and the line says how many. If nobody answers in time, the ask also goes to every admin.

### Team review of Production deletes

Once a workspace has a second person, a Delete or Irreversible action in Production (confirmed, or not placed yet and treated as Production) also goes to the Production approvers. The requester can still answer it themselves unless someone else must approve; the first answer counts.

### Someone else must approve

With **Someone other than the requester must approve** on:

- The person whose agent asked can't approve their own ask, in Passport, Slack, a notification, or their agent's own prompt.
- Any one of the approvers is enough. The ask shows who it needs, like "Needs Maya or Sam".
- Nothing it covers runs on its own in Production, whatever anyone's rules say. Blocks still block.
- Undoing a covered Production action needs an approver too.
- When Passport only guessed where an ask runs, its requester can say it's Production but not anything looser. An approver who's an admin can place it with **It's Staging, always run**.

## Exception requests

When a team or workspace rule blocks something a member needs, their agent can ask to lift it, with a one-line reason: for that one tool, for an hour or a day. An irreversible action can only be asked for as "ask first", never "run". The request is filed for the member, names the agent that wrote it, and waits for owners under **Waiting on you** on [Work](/member/work) as a **Rule request**.

**Review** shows the request in one sentence, the reason, what it lifts, and where. **Approve** (optionally changing how long) adds a rule just for that person that lifts the one rule for that one action. **Decline** takes an optional note. Team minimums and Production approvers still apply. The member gets an email either way.

## Weekly summary

Every Monday, people who had agent activity get a short email: what Passport handled for them, what they approved, and what's new. Admins get the workspace version. Turn it off with **Weekly summary email** in [Settings › Notifications](https://passportmcp.com/#settings/notifications).
