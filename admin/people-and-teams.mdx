Open in Passport: [People](https://passportmcp.com/#people)

**People** is in the menu under your name, for owners on Pro and Enterprise. It lists who's in the workspace and what their agents can do.

## Invite people

**Invite people** is the page's one action. Paste or type addresses, as many as you like, in any shape: one per line, separated by commas or spaces, or `Name <address>`. Pick a role, **Member** or **Admin**, and a team if you have teams, then invite. Each person gets an email with a link to join, or, when Passport can't send email, you get a sign-in link to share.

Each invite is tied to the address it was sent to and expires after 14 days. The email also has one line to set up agents from a terminal, `npx passport-bridge init --org <key>`; an invitee who signs in or runs it with the invited address joins without the link.

**Let anyone with your company email join**, under the form, opens **Settings › Workspace**. See [Who can join](#who-can-join).

## Waiting to join

**Waiting to join**, at the top of the list, holds join requests and pending invites:

- A **join request** has **Approve**, which adds them as a member and emails a sign-in link, and **Deny request**, which asks first. If the email can't be delivered, Passport says so; they're still approved.
- A pending **invite** has **Resend**, which sends a fresh link and stops the old one, and **Revoke invite**, which asks first.

## The list

Filter by team (**All teams** or one team) and by status: **Active**, **Invited**, or **Deactivated**. Each row shows a person's role, team, how many agents they have, and when they were last active. **Manage teams** creates, renames, and deletes teams.

## A person's page

Open a person for their page:

- **Agents**: their agents, with **Needs sign-in** or **Not used yet** when that applies.
- **Work**: their recent tasks, and **All their work** to see the rest in Work.
- **Access**: their **Role** and **Team**, each saved as you change it. Then **Deactivate** or **Reactivate**, and **Remove**.

## Roles

| Role | Can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Manage apps, people, rules, and the workspace. The first person in a workspace is an owner. |
| **Member** | Connect their agents and use the apps they have access to. |

Invites call the owner role **Admin**. A workspace always keeps at least one owner: Passport won't let the last one step down, be deactivated, or be removed. Changing someone's team so their Production minimum gets looser takes a passkey check. See [Confirm it's you](/member/safety#confirm-its-you).

## Teams

A team groups people so apps and rules can apply to all of them: **Who can use it** on an app's page, and team minimums and **Require the guard** on **Rules › Everyone**. Renaming a team keeps its access and rules. Deleting one asks first.

## Who can join

Free and Pro workspaces are invite-only. On Enterprise, **Settings › Workspace › Joining** can let anyone who verifies an email on your company domains join as a member. You can also copy the workspace's sign-in link there.

<Note>
  Consumer and disposable email domains never let anyone join by domain, so shared providers like gmail.com can't be
  used to walk into your workspace.
</Note>

## Deactivate and reactivate

Deactivating someone signs them out everywhere right away, stops agent tokens they own, and disconnects their connected accounts, so nothing re-arms later. Reactivating gives access back; they reconnect their own accounts. **Remove** deletes them and their access settings, and can't be undone.

<Note>
  The same lifecycle runs through SCIM. Deprovisioning someone in your identity provider does exactly this, and Passport
  refuses to remove the last owner. See [SCIM](/enterprise/scim).
</Note>

## Automation

Scripts and CI use agent tokens, not people. Admins create them with **Add agent › A script or job** on Agents. A token belongs to the person who created it, and deactivating that person revokes it. See [Agent tokens](/member/agents#agent-tokens).
