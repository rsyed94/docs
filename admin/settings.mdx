Open in Passport: [Settings](https://passportmcp.com/#settings)

**Settings** is in the menu under your name, at the bottom left. Everyone gets **Account** and **Notifications**. Admins also get **Workspace** and **Billing**. Each part has its own address, such as [`#settings/workspace`](https://passportmcp.com/#settings/workspace).

What agents may do lives in [Rules](/member/safety), not here. Keys Passport holds for you live on each app's page, and agent tokens on their agent's page.

## Account

- Your name, role, and workspace.
- **Theme**: **Light**, **Dark**, or **System**.
- **Security**: your passkeys, which Passport uses to [confirm it's you](/member/safety#confirm-its-you). Without one, Passport emails you a code.
- **Signed-in browsers**: every browser signed in as you, each with **Sign out**, and **Sign out everywhere else**. Signing out never stops your agents.

## Notifications

- **Approval alerts in this browser**: a notification for each ask that waits on you. Allow notifications when your browser asks, then use **Send test** to check it reaches this device. Each browser needs its own permission. On iPhone or iPad, add Passport to your Home Screen, open it from there, and turn alerts on there.
- **Slack**: an admin adds the Passport app with **Add to Slack**. After that, asks arrive as direct messages, and each person can turn Slack messages off for themselves. Admins can also pick a **Production channel** where Production asks are posted. See [Approvals in Slack](/admin/slack-and-approvers#approvals-in-slack).
- **Weekly summary email**: every Monday, what Passport handled for you, what you approved, and what's new. Admins get the workspace version.

A notification carries a generic reminder, never the action's arguments or account details. Open it to review the ask in Passport. If it expired or someone already answered, Passport shows that instead of taking a second answer.

## Workspace

For admins:

- **Workspace name**.
- **Joining**: **Copy sign-in link**, and a link to People. On Enterprise, **Who can join**: invite-only, or anyone who verifies an email on your company domains. See [Who can join](/admin/people-and-teams#who-can-join).
- **Single sign-on and directory** (Enterprise): [single sign-on (OIDC)](/enterprise/sso-oidc) and [SCIM provisioning](/enterprise/scim).
- **Record and backup**:
  - **Show what agents say they're doing** (off by default): Claude Code's one-line description of each command becomes its label in Work. The description is shortened on the computer, and one containing anything that looks like a key, token, or email address is never sent. Passport keeps it for 90 days. Turning it off stops new ones and hides the ones it kept.
  - **Token usage per task** (on by default): Claude Code tasks show their tokens and API-equivalent cost. Only numbers leave the computer. See [token usage](/member/cli-hook#token-usage).
  - **Stream to OpenTelemetry** and **Stream to your SIEM** (Enterprise). See [SIEM and OTLP export](/enterprise/siem-export).
  - **Workspace backup**: the whole workspace as one JSON file. It holds encrypted credentials, so store it as a secret.
- **Danger zone**: **Delete this workspace**, after typing its name.

## Billing

For admins: your plan, people and automation counts, and the trial if one is running. Upgrade through Stripe Checkout, or change plans and cancel through the Stripe customer portal. A self-hosted Passport has no Billing. See [Plans and billing](/billing).
