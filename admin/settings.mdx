Open in Passport: [Settings](https://passportmcp.com/#settings)

**Settings** sits at the bottom of the sidebar, and the account menu under your name opens **Account settings** too. Everyone gets **Account**. Admins on Pro and Enterprise also get **Workspace**, **Access**, **Billing**, and **Advanced**. Each tab has its own address, such as [`#settings/workspace`](https://passportmcp.com/#settings/workspace), so a link opens the right one.

Rules don't live here. What agents may do is on [Rules](/member/safety), and workspace policy is on **Rules › Team**.

## Account

Your own controls, on every plan:

- **Notifications**: **Approval alerts in this browser** (see [browser notifications](/admin/approvals-and-requests#browser-approval-notifications)), **Weekly summary email**, and **Slack**. An admin adds Passport to Slack here with **Add to Slack**; then each person can turn **Approval requests in Slack** off for themselves. See [Approvals in Slack](/admin/slack-and-approvers#approvals-in-slack).
- **Appearance**: light, dark, or follow your system.
- **Where you're signed in**: every browser and computer signed in to Passport, each with a way to sign it out.

On Free, the owner's Account tab also has **Billing** and a **Workspace** card. **Manage workspace** there opens the [workspace page](https://passportmcp.com/#workspace): its name, invitations, ownership, and, under **Advanced**, **Workspace backup** and account lifecycle.

## Keys moved

Settings has no Keys tab. The vendor keys Passport holds for you, such as Railway or GitHub, live on each app's page, in its **Keys** section, even for an app you haven't added; **Apps › Other keys** holds keys for a CLI Passport doesn't know (see [Keys in Passport](/member/cli-hook#keys-in-passport)). Agent tokens (`pak_…`) live with their agents on [Agents](/admin/client-fleet#manage-your-own-agents), and enrollment keys (`pek_…`) on [Agents › Rollout](/admin/rollout#enrollment-keys). Old links to the Keys tab (`#settings/keys`, `#keys`, and each `?section=`) open where those keys live and say so.

## Workspace

- **Workspace name**.
- **Sessions**: [**Show what agents say they're doing**](/admin/activity-and-audit#what-agents-say-theyre-doing) (off by default), and **Token usage per session** (on by default; see [token usage](/member/cli-hook#token-usage)).
- **Admin activity**: **View activity** opens every privileged change, such as access policy, approvals, team edits, data checks, and revocations, with who did it and when. See [Admin activity](/admin/activity-and-audit#admin-activity).
- **Workspace backup**: download a full backup for recovery and support.

## Access

- **Joining**: **Copy sign-in link**, a link to **People**, and on Enterprise, who may join: invite-only, or anyone who verifies an email on your **Company email domains**, and whether the workspace is **Discoverable by your team**. See [Who can join](/admin/people-and-teams#who-can-join).
- **Which agents can connect**: a link to [Rules › Team](https://passportmcp.com/#rules/team?section=agents), where it's set.
- **Keys in Passport**: how many days before a key asks to be rotated (90 by default).
- **Approval defaults**: a link to [Rules › Team](https://passportmcp.com/#rules/team?section=floors) for team floors and who approves Production deletes.

## Billing

Your plan, people and automation counts, and the trial if one is running. Upgrade with Stripe Checkout, or change plans and cancel through the Stripe customer portal. See [Plans and billing](/billing).

## Advanced

- **Alerts**: an **Alert webhook** that gets a one-line message when a data check fires or an app's tools change, with a test send.
- **Enterprise**: [single sign-on (OIDC)](/enterprise/sso-oidc), [SCIM provisioning](/enterprise/scim), exports of Activity (CSV and JSONL), and [OTLP and SIEM export](/enterprise/siem-export). A self-hosted Passport also shows **Secrets at rest**.
- **Danger zone**: **Delete this workspace**, after typing its name.

## Where things moved

| Was | Now |
| --- | --- |
| Settings › Admin activity | **Settings › Workspace › Admin activity** |
| Settings › Safety | [Rules](https://passportmcp.com/#rules) (admins: **Rules › Team**) |
| Settings › Access › Which agents can connect | **Rules › Team › Which agents can connect** |
| Settings › Automation | **Agents**, filtered to **Tokens** |
| The Keys tab in Settings: keys Passport holds for you, Apps › Keys | Each app's **Keys** section (**Apps › Other keys** for a CLI Passport doesn't know) |
| The Keys tab in Settings: agent tokens | **Agents**, filtered to **Tokens**; each token is on its agent's page |
| The Keys tab in Settings: enrollment keys | **Agents › Rollout** |
| Rules › Environments, Rules › Project scripts | Each app's **Environments** and **Project scripts** sections (**Apps › Other projects** for scripts no known app hosts) |

Old links to these places open the new one.
