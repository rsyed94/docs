On Enterprise, admins can see AI assistants across the whole workspace, revoke connections, and require only recognized clients. Free and Pro members manage their own Clients page. On Enterprise, personal devices live under **Clients → My clients**. Company-wide fleet rules are kept if you downgrade, but they are not enforced until Enterprise is active again.

## See workspace clients

On Enterprise, open **Clients** and use the **Workspace clients** tab. You can search, open a person, spot stale clients, and revoke a device so it must sign in again.

Anyone can revoke their own devices from **Clients** (or **My clients** on Enterprise). Listing everyone and admin revoke need Enterprise.

## Require registered clients

Under **Settings**, in the **AI client access** card, Enterprise admins can:

- **Allow dynamic clients** — whether unrecognized clients may connect
- **Require registered AI clients** — only clients Passport recognizes (or you have registered) may connect

On Free and Pro, policy stays open even if older Enterprise settings are still stored.

## Team defaults

[Client defaults](/admin/client-defaults) choose which allowed apps appear by default in a given AI assistant for a team. They do not grant new access — passes still decide what someone can reach.
