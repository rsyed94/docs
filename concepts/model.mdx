Open in Passport: [Work](https://passportmcp.com/#work)

Passport sits between an agent and the work app it wants to use. It keeps the app's credential, checks access and rules, makes the call, and records what happened.

## The words to know

### Workspace

Your team's private space in Passport. It holds people, apps, rules, and the record. Workspaces are isolated from one another.

### App

Something your agents use through Passport, such as GitHub, Linear, Stripe, or Notion. Some apps connect through MCP, others through a reviewed API driver; Passport treats them the same. An app is also its vendor's CLI, so Railway's tools, the `railway` commands agents run through `passport exec`, and the Railway key you keep in Passport all belong to the Railway app. See [Apps](/member/apps).

### Tool

What an app exposes, such as "create issue" or "delete branch". Each tool is one class of action: **Read**, **Change**, **Delete**, or **Irreversible**.

### Agent

Where you use AI: Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, Claude.ai, ChatGPT, a cloud agent, or a script with an agent token. See [Agents](/member/agents).

### Task

One run of one agent, such as a Claude Code session, with every action it took. Tasks live on [Work](/member/work).

### Rule

What an agent may do without asking, per environment: run, ask you first, or never run. See [Rules](/member/safety).

## Access

Access answers one question: what may this person do with this app?

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Full access" icon="unlock">
    Every tool, Read through Irreversible.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Read-only" icon="glasses">
    Only Read tools. The rest are hidden from agents.
  </Card>
  <Card title="No access" icon="ban">
    The app isn't shown to that person.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Admins set it under **Who can use it** on each app's page, for everyone or, on Pro and Enterprise, per team and person. Access comes before every rule.

## What happens during a call

<Steps>
  <Step title="Identify the person and agent">
    Passport checks who is signed in and which agent is calling.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply access, then rules">
    Apps and tools the person may not use are hidden and blocked, and no rule can change that. Then rules run, block,
    or hold the action for an answer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Inspect the request">Data checks inspect what the agent sends before Passport contacts the app.</Step>
  <Step title="Call the app">Passport uses the connected account on the person's behalf.</Step>
  <Step title="Inspect and record the result">
    Data checks inspect the result, then Passport records the action and returns the safe response.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## People and roles

- **Owners** manage apps, people, rules, and the workspace. On Pro and Enterprise they get **People** and can switch Work, Agents, and Rules to **Everyone**. On Free, the one person in the workspace is its admin.
- **Members** connect agents and use the apps available to them.
- **Teams** let owners give a group the same access and rules.
- **Agent tokens** connect scripts and CI. Each belongs to the person who created it.

Deactivating a person signs them out, stops their agent tokens, and disconnects their accounts right away.
